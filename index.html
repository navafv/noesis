About Noesis'26

Noesis'26 is a premier National-Level Inter-College IT Fest hosted by the Neura IT Club, Department of Computer Science, at Jamia Hamdard Kannur Campus, Kerala. Over two days, students from across the country compete in hackathons, technical contests, gaming showdowns, and creative challenges spanning coding, design, AI, and digital media.

Events & Prize Pools

  • Coding — Competitive programming (C/C++/Java/Python). Prize pool: ₹10,000. Day 1, 10:30 AM, Computer Lab A.
  • Debugging — Bug-hunting sprint against the clock. Prize pool: ₹7,000. Day 1, 01:30 PM, Computer Lab B.
  • Web Designing — Surprise-theme responsive UI build, team of 2. Prize pool: ₹8,000. Day 1, 02:30 PM, Multimedia Lab.
  • Online Treasure Hunt — Ciphers, steganography & campus-wide clue trail. Prize pool: ₹6,000. Day 1, 04:00 PM, Online / Campus-wide.
  • AI Prompting — AI Prompt Engineering contest across text and image models. Prize pool: ₹6,000. Day 2, 10:00 AM, Seminar Hall.
  • IT Quiz — Tech trivia and buzzer finale, team of 2. Prize pool: ₹8,000. Day 2, 11:30 AM, Main Auditorium.
  • Gaming / Esports — Valorant, BGMI & FIFA brackets. Prize pool: ₹12,000. Day 2, 01:30 PM, Gaming Arena / Audi.
  • Blind Typing — High-WPM typing test with monitors off. Prize pool: ₹4,000. Day 2, 03:00 PM, Lab A.
  • Reel Making — 60-second Instagram reel capturing the fest. Prize pool: ₹5,000. Submission by Day 2, 03:30 PM, Campus-wide.
